I developed a land ownership map (contact me for access) focussed on Cambridge to better inform my belief that the landownership pattern of Cambridge's environs and each landowner's appetite for development had affected and will affect its growth pattern. I have continued to add to it, with a focus on landownership around large cities and of the largest landowners. There are now over 90 estates/large farms mapped, covering over 210,000 acres.
